# Swimming Physiology Research

## 1. Abstract

This independent research project aims to investigate the relationships between blood lactate, training intensity, recovery and swimming performance. By collecting physiological and performance data over an extended period, the study will explore patterns and relationships that may help improve the understanding of individual training responses and swimming performance. Statistical analysis and machine learning will be explored to identify useful trends and support more informed training decisions.

## 2. Research Methodology

The project will follow a longitudinal observational approach, collecting physiological and swimming performance data over several months.

Data collected may include:

- Blood lactate concentrations
- Heart rate and recovery measurements
- Swimming times and split times
- Training intensity, duration and distance
- Other relevant training and performance variables

The collected data will be organised, processed and analysed using Python. Statistical methods, including descriptive statistics, correlation and regression, will be used to investigate relationships between variables. Machine learning methods may also be explored where the amount and quality of data justify their use.

Data collection procedures will aim to remain consistent to improve the reliability and comparability of measurements.

## 3. Expected Outcomes

The research aims to:

- Identify relationships between lactate, training intensity, recovery and swimming performance.
- Better understand physiological responses to different training sessions.
- Identify patterns in performance and recovery over time.
- Explore how statistical analysis and machine learning can provide useful insights into training.
- Develop findings that may help inform training methods and support optimal swimming performance.
